Greece

Historical Culture

Greece is the cradle of Western civilization, where democracy was born. Ancient temples and archaeological sites tell stories of gods and heroes.

Modern Innovations

Contemporary Greece harmoniously blends ancient heritage with modern progress. The Stavros Niarchos Foundation represents architectural innovation.

Interesting Traditions

Greek traditions span thousands of years. Easter celebrations feature unique customs like the Holy Friday processions.

Beautiful Nature

Greece's diverse landscapes range from pristine beaches to majestic mountains. Crystal-clear waters surround over 6,000 islands.

Unique Language

Greek is one of the world's oldest recorded living languages. The Greek alphabet developed around 800 BC.

Amazing People

Greeks are known for their warm hospitality and strong family values. The concept of "philoxenia" means love for strangers.

Athens - Where Ancient Meets Modern

As one of the world's oldest cities, Athens is a living museum of classical civilization. From the iconic Parthenon overlooking the city to the vibrant Plaka neighborhood with its cobblestone streets, Athens offers a unique blend of history and contemporary culture.

Explore world-class museums, enjoy traditional tavernas, and experience the famous Greek hospitality in this magnificent city that shaped Western civilization.

The Beauty of Greece

Santorini Sunset

Embrace the unparalleled beauty: A Santorini sunset is a magical moment when the sky ignites with shades of orange, pink, and gold, creating a breathtaking spectacle.

Experience the special atmosphere of Oia: The best place to witness the sunset is the village of Oia, where the white-washed houses and blue-domed churches create a perfect contrast with the captivating colors of the sky.

A moment of romance and serenity: This is the ideal time for couples to enjoy a romantic atmosphere that hovers over the island as the sun dips below the horizon.

Capture unforgettable photos: Santorini at sunset is a dream for photographers, as every shot turns into a true masterpiece.

Feel the unique magic: This moment isn't just the sun disappearing below the horizon; it's the culmination of the day, leaving an unforgettable memory for a lifetime.

Acropolis of Athens

The Acropolis of Athens is a UNESCO World Heritage site and the most famous landmark in Greece, symbolizing the birthplace of democracy and philosophy. Located on a rocky hill, it served as a fortified religious center dedicated to the goddess Athena. The site is home to several masterpieces of classical Greek architecture, including the Parthenon, a monumental temple to Athena, and the Erechtheion, known for its famous Porch of the Caryatids. The Propylaea serves as the grand entrance. The best time to visit is early in the morning to avoid crowds and heat. A visit to the nearby Acropolis Museum is highly recommended to see the artifacts found on the site.

Mykonos Town

Mykonos Town, or Chora, is the vibrant capital of Mykonos. It's famous for its labyrinthine, narrow streets and distinctive Cycladic architecture—white-washed houses with colorful doors and balconies. The town is a mix of high-end boutiques and a lively nightlife scene. Key attractions include Little Venice, with its houses built directly on the sea; the Windmills, a historical landmark offering great views; and the picturesque Panagia Paraportiani Church. Mykonos Town is best explored on foot, and it beautifully balances a bustling atmosphere with hidden, tranquil corners.

Mediterranean Cuisine

FMediterranean Cuisine Mediterranean cuisine is a culinary tradition from countries around the Mediterranean Sea, known for its simplicity and health benefits. It's based on three main staples: olives, wheat, and grapes, which provide oil, bread, and wine. The diet is rich in plant-based foods (vegetables, fruits, legumes), healthy fats (olive oil, nuts), and lean proteins (fish, seafood). Red meat is consumed rarely. Dishes are flavored with fresh herbs like oregano, basil, and rosemary.

Navagio Beach

Navagio Beach, also known as Shipwreck Beach, is one of the most iconic and photogenic locations in Greece, situated on the island of Zakynthos. The beach is world-famous for its stunning white limestone cliffs, crystal-clear turquoise waters, and of course, the rusted shipwreck resting on the sand.
